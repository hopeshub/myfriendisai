# About-page disclosure copy — draft (2026-05-15)

Proposed additions to `web/app/about/page.tsx` arising from the keyword context
spot-check audit (`docs/keyword_context_spotcheck_2026-05-15.md`). Written to
match the existing editorial first-person voice. **Draft only — not applied to
the live page.** Numbers reflect the completed n=100 confirmatory read: therapy
theme ~64% topical precision, its two weakest keywords (`emotional support`,
`therapeutic`) ~55%; `goodbye` cleared at 81%.

---

## 1. New limit — add to the "How to read the lines" section

Add as a fourth bold-lead paragraph after "Each line is a floor, not a ceiling":

> **Some lines are noisier than others.** A line is only as clean as the words
> that feed it, and those words are not equally clean. The sexual-content and
> addiction lines are tight — their vocabulary is distinctive and rarely means
> anything else. The therapy and consciousness lines are looser: their words
> — "emotional support," "therapeutic," "personhood" — are ordinary enough to
> turn up in posts that are really about something else. When I re-read a large
> sample of tagged posts with independent classifiers in May 2026, the strong
> themes held up well, but roughly a third of the therapy-tagged posts turned
> out to be about something else — a passing mention, a different theme, or
> someone else's experience. Read therapy and consciousness as rough indicators
> that a subject is being talked about, not as measurements of how much.

## 2. Revised "Data & code" paragraph — replace the "without a seam" sentence

The current text says the archive and live-collection stretches "join without
a seam." That is true of the *format* but not of *coverage*. Replace the first
paragraph of "Data & code" with:

> Posts from 2017 through early 2026 were backfilled from public Reddit
> archives (PullPush and Arctic Shift). From March 2026 onward, they are
> collected fresh from Reddit every day. One caveat comes with the older years:
> the further back a post goes, the more likely its text was removed or deleted
> before the archive captured it — so there is simply less wording for the
> keywords to match in the early years than in the recent ones. Every line
> therefore runs a little lower at its start than the discourse really was,
> which makes each rise look somewhat steeper than it was. The shape and the
> timing of events are sound; the steepness of the long climb is partly the
> instrument warming up, not only the subject growing.

## 3. Changelog entry

Add to the changelog (most-recent end):

> **2026-05-15 — Spot-check audit.** Re-read ~1,800 tagged posts with
> independent classifiers to check that keywords land on the theme they claim.
> The finding: keywords reliably identify AI-companion discourse, but sort it
> into the right theme unevenly — strong for sex/ERP and addiction, weak for
> therapy and consciousness. Added the precision caveat above and the
> early-years caveat to "Data & code." No keyword changes: the response to a
> fast-moving vocabulary is disclosure and monitoring, not constant edits.
