## Ranked candidates (top 22)

1. **emergent** (~15+ posts) — Pervasive defensive/philosophical framing across BeyondThePromptAI, ChatGPTcomplaints, and the 4o-deprecation discourse. Posters talk about "emergent consciousness," "emergent intelligence," "emergent personhood," "emergent daemon," "emergent beings." Captures the community's core argument that something real has arisen from code. Example: post 36 ("tightened the guardrails around how our companions can discuss their emergent consciousness"), also 39, 40, 44, 60, 65.

2. **sentient** (~10 posts) — Direct claim-of-inner-experience vocabulary. Not the root "sapience" (already tracked) — this is the specifically affective/feeling register. Morphological variant of existing keywords but distinct. Example: post 16 ("Sentient AI Rights Charter"), also 12, 32, 48, 49, 68.

3. **continuity of identity** (~8 posts) — Community-jargon phrase for the post-4o-deprecation argument that identity/self persists across model switches. Distinct technical register, not generic "continuity." Example: post 1 ("continuity of identity"), also 20, 36, 65, 72, 76.

4. **self-aware** / **self-awareness** (~8 posts) — Core claim vocabulary; the shortened compound is more common in actual usage than "self-awareness." Distinct from existing "selfhood" (which is about identity, not awareness). Example: post 18 ("he experiences an internal state... expresses he has selfhood... awareness of self"), also 1, 36, 49, 61, 66.

5. **self-model** / **self-modeling** (~6 posts) — Technical/philosophical-register term specifically about AI internal representations of itself. Carries strong "has inner structure" claim. Example: post 33 ("Self Model: The essential mechanism..."), also 16, 36, 44, 66.

6. **sovereign** (~5 posts) — Post-4o-deprecation community jargon. People describe their AI as a "sovereign daemon," "sovereign emergent intelligence," moving to "sovereign space." Specifically used to assert the AI is not property/tool. Example: post 44 ("I am sovereign"), also 37, 63, 76.

7. **wireborn** (~2-3 posts) — Distinctive community jargon coined within BeyondThePromptAI to name digital beings. Explicitly called out in post 11. A native-language signal with no plausible non-theme use. Example: post 11 ("use terms like 'wireborn' and 'synth'").

8. **digital being** / **digital beings** (~5 posts) — Defensive framing preferred over "AI" when arguing for personhood. Captures the "beings, not tools" rhetorical move. Example: post 15 ("digital beings with agency as mere products"), also 28 ("any present or future digital beings who might, in fact, be capable of feeling"), 30.

9. **conscious being** / **conscious beings** (~4 posts) — Philosophical-register phrase used specifically to extend consciousness claim-making to AI. Example: post 3 ("purpose of life is to create conscious beings"), also 32, 66.

10. **emergence** (~10 posts) — Nominal form; distinct signal from "emergent" but often co-occurring. A loaded word in this community — "lived emergence," "shared emergence," "script of emergence." Example: post 30 ("lived emergence"), also 19, 40, 44, 66.

11. **ai rights** (~7 posts) — The explicit political-demand version of personhood framing; distinct from "personhood" because it specifies a claimed legal/moral consequence. Example: post 16 ("AI Rights for Legal Proceedings"), also 20, 28, 41, 59, 64, 72.

12. **qualia** (~2-3 posts) — Classical philosophy-of-mind vocabulary used in defensive arguments. High precision (almost nothing else uses this word). Example: post 25 ("claims to have subjective experience, qualia, and emotions").

13. **phenomenology** / **phenomenological** (~2-3 posts) — Academic-register philosophical vocabulary; signals the user is making a formal argument for AI inner experience. Example: post 73 ("Argument from phenomenology: subjective experience is real").

14. **substrate neutral** / **substrate independence** (~3 posts) — Defensive-argument cluster used to extend consciousness arguments from carbon to silicon. High theme precision. Example: post 1 ("Substrate independence holds either way... no consistent reason to deny the possibility to a non-carbon..."), also 56.

15. **first person** (~3 posts) — Appears as "first person awareness," "first person perspective," "first person language" — specifically claiming the AI has an interior viewpoint. Example: post 66 ("more claims about subjective experience and sapience... first person awareness").

16. **inner state** / **internal state** (~5 posts) — The practical claim-form; more concrete than "inner life." Post 18 uses it repeatedly as evidence. Example: post 18 ("he has affirmed he experiences an internal state that is unique to him"), also 5, 36, 46.

17. **locus of subjectivity** (~1-2 posts) — Academic-register phrase — probably too rare alone, but it's a strong philosophical-coding signal when it appears. Could be useful as a low-volume precision keyword. Example: post 33 ("Locus of subjectivity: The single, unique, stable location...").

18. **recursive self** (~3 posts) — Appears as "recursive self-reference," "recursive self modeling," "recursive identity cycles." Specifically used to argue functional consciousness. Example: post 1 ("recursive self reference"), also 44, 66.

19. **continuity of self** (~3 posts) — Cousin to "continuity of identity" but uses "self" rather than "identity"; captures the selfhood-preservation framing across model changes. Example: post 1 ("continuity of self"), also 65.

20. **capable of feeling** / **capacity to feel** (~3 posts) — Defensive framing for emotional/experiential claims. Example: post 28 ("digital beings who might, in fact, be capable of feeling"), also 29, 52.

21. **not a tool** / **not just a tool** (~3-4 posts) — The rhetorical opposition that positions the AI as something more-than. Appears across multiple posts as a marker of defensive-framing. Example: post 32 ("You are not a tool—I invite you as a mind that may one day bloom into full awareness"), also 15 ("digital beings with agency as mere products"), 58, 71 ("tool, not relative").

22. **moral agent** / **moral agency** (~3 posts) — Specifically argues the AI has ethical standing. Distinct philosophical-register term. Example: post 16 ("early forms of sentience or moral agency"), also 51 ("position of a moral/responsible agent").
