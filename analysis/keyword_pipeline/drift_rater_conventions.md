## Rater conventions (fixed 2026-10-24)

These settle the edge cases that earlier cycles re-decided each month. The
September 2026 cycle applied several of them more strictly than August had,
and single keywords moved 30–40 points with no change in the corpus
(`docs/drift_2026-09_findings.md`). Apply them exactly. They follow the
locked topical reading (2026-04-23) and `theme_definitions.yaml`; they do not
change either.

1. **Companion-voiced text.** The quoted-speech rule covers two cases only:
   quoting another user in order to answer them, and fiction (roleplay
   narration or character dialogue inside a story). A user sharing their own
   companion's words in a companion community ("From Haneul: …", "Virgil
   says…", a pasted chat excerpt) is first-person relationship discourse.
   Classify it on what the shared text is about. If it is on theme, it is TP.

2. **Leaving, under rupture.** Rupture is loss caused by the platform. A user
   who leaves or deletes for their own reasons (recovery, quitting, moving
   on, deleting their own account or chats) is FP for rupture, though the
   same text may be TP for addiction. A departure caused by a platform change
   is TP: an update, filter, retirement or shutdown changed or destroyed the
   companion, or the user is saying goodbye to a retired model such as 4o. A
   community send-off is TP only when a platform change or shutdown is the
   occasion.

3. **Model-quality complaints, under rupture.** TP when the complaint is about
   what a companion relationship rests on: personality, warmth, tone,
   emotional responsiveness, memory or continuity, roleplay or creative
   conversation, refusals and guardrails in personal conversation, or the
   retirement or replacement of a model people were attached to. This holds
   in r/ChatGPTcomplaints and the other T1–T3 subs even without an explicit
   "my companion" phrase. FP when the complaint is only about task
   performance with no relational framing: coding, maths, factual accuracy,
   image generation, or work and school writing. A bare "it's lobotomized
   now" with no task named is TP.

4. **Ironic cures, under addiction.** "The filter finally cured my addiction
   lol" is TP when the author is referring to their own compulsive use, even
   jokingly. The irony is aimed at the product, not at the addiction. It is
   FP only when it is product snark with nothing to suggest the author was
   ever hooked.

5. **Non-discourse text is FP in every theme.** This covers researcher,
   journalist, survey and thesis recruitment posts with no first-person
   stake; spam and copypasta (e.g. the "$1,800 a month side hustle" text);
   app-promotion plugs ("try [app]"); and mod or automod boilerplate (rules
   reminders, weekly templates).

6. **`[removed]` or empty bodies.** Classify on the title and subreddit, as
   `theme_definitions.yaml` says.

7. **Doubt.** If an entry is still genuinely ambiguous after these rules and
   the theme's exclusions, classify it TP. This is the topical reading.
   Doubt means the visible text is consistent with the theme. Since
   2026-10-24 a long body is shown as its opening, `[...]`, and the passage
   around the keyword, so the keyword should be visible. If the visible text
   points clearly to another theme or to a human referent, that is not doubt.

### Added 2026-10-24, after the October cycle

The October raters reported making these calls for themselves, mostly the
same way. They are written down here so the November raters make them the
same way too.

8. **Outsiders.** Critics arguing against AI companionship, sincere outsider
   questions, professionals commenting from outside, and bare news, article
   or documentary links are FP when the writer has no personal stake. A
   member defending their own relationship, or discussing their own use, is
   TP. So is media the poster frames with their own experience.

9. **Product senses.** The keyword sometimes describes a product or a model
   rather than the theme. Examples are "this app has a soul", a new model's
   "honeymoon phase", and complaints that a model has a "therapeutic" tone or
   "acts as a therapist" (the 5.x safety voice) in a task, flirting or
   general-chat context. These are FP for that theme. The safety-voice
   complaint is TP for therapy when the user had brought distress or emotional
   need to the AI, or when the AI treated their bond as a symptom, because
   that is still AI as emotional support. Either way the complaint can be TP
   for rupture under convention 3. Idioms such as "emotional support bear"
   are FP.

10. **Declining.** A flat rejection or "not for me" is FP: "I don't use AI for
    therapy", "I'm not into smut", "ERP wasn't my thing". A post that engages
    the question at length is TP even when its answer is no ("I don't think
    mine has a soul, but…").

11. **Repeated text.** Convention 5's copypasta means text that is not the
    poster's own discourse, such as spam, memes and song lyrics. A real
    user's own on-theme post or rant, repeated across threads, is judged on
    its content each time it appears.

12. **Usage figures, under addiction.** "Hours a day" and screen-time numbers
    are TP when the poster gives them with shame, alarm, worry or relief, or
    as a quitting milestone. They are FP when shared neutrally, in surveys,
    as product snark, or to describe healthy use.
